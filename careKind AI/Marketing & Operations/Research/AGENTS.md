# Research scope

Use the relevant market/compliance shared skill and workflow. README.md indexes current reports.

- Cite sources and retrieval/version dates; distinguish official rules, vendor statements, independent results and inference.
- For product design/PRD work, apply the company `rules/product-and-legal.md` and actually consult relevant local legal/reference content.
- Preserve downloaded official originals in References/ and maintain provenance; save interpretation separately.
- Do not claim missing capabilities from absent webpage text, or describe an unverified legal requirement as universally applicable.
