# Marketing and operations scope

Company procedures, marketing, customers and research belong here. Shared product scope is in `../docs/PRD_careKind_AI.md`.

- Apply `../rules/operations.md` and the relevant shared skill/workflow.
- Research claims need dated sources and a distinction between supplier statements, evidence and inference.
- `Research/References/` holds original official downloads. Preserve originals and maintain provenance; local research is not a legal approval.
- Changes to product promises must agree with the PRD and actual evidence. Drafting external communications does not authorise sending them.
- Company OS records actual responsibility/approval status; do not infer appointments from team titles.
