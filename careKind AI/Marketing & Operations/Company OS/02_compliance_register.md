# Australian Compliance Register

Working register, not legal advice or a compliance certification. Applicability depends on actual product functions, entity/customer roles, data flows and operating jurisdictions. Aged care providers retain care-delivery obligations; a software vendor may have separate privacy, consumer, contract and TGA duties. Have Australian counsel confirm the position.

| Area / official source | Relevance and action | Evidence to retain | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) | Health information is sensitive. Health service providers can be covered even if small. Assess whether careKind AI holds health information, provides a health service, or processes for a customer; document roles and lawful purposes. | Data map; privacy impact assessment; notices; agreements; access/correction process; retention schedule | Privacy | Open |

OAIC best practice is not to put sensitive personal information into public generative AI tools. | Notice; purpose register; vendor/subprocessor locations; transfer review; safeguards; deletion evidence; model terms | Privacy + Security | Open |
| Notifiable Data Breaches scheme | Determine quickly whether a suspected breach is eligible and likely to cause serious harm; statutory assessment is generally up to 30 days. Notify OAIC and affected people when required. Customer contract may require faster notice. | Incident record; assessment; rationale; notifications and correspondence | Privacy | Open |
| Aged Care Act 2024 (Cth), Aged Care Rules 2025, strengthened Quality Standards | New Act and strengthened standards applied from 1 November 2025. Map customer workflows to applicable rights, governance, care, clinical-care and records outcomes. Do not present the software as certifying a provider's compliance. | Requirements mapping; user validation; product traceability; training and audit evidence | Product + Clinical | Open |

Drafting/transcription may have a different status from analysis, prompts or recommendations. ## Mandatory release decision

Before customer supply, record a signed assessment of intended purpose; TGA status and any conditions; sponsor/manufacturer responsibilities; privacy roles and data flows; state/territory requirements; customer contract; and incident/post-market routes. Reassess when functionality, claims, model or data flow changes.
