## Purpose

Help aged care nurses spend less time on repetitive paperwork and more time with older people, while supporting clear, timely, reviewable documentation.

## Principles

- Respect each older person's dignity, rights, preferences, identity and privacy.
- Keep nurses responsible for clinical judgement and final records.
- Design with nurses, older people and representatives; validate accessibility, accents, speech impairment and noisy care environments.
- Be transparent about AI use, product limits, data handling and evidence.
- Measure safety and quality alongside time saved.

## Provisional product thesis

A B2B assistant that processes authorised aged-care shift recordings (for example, eight hours), organises facts by resident, maintains a traceable care-event timeline and creates care-note drafts for human review and approval. Uncertain resident attribution remains pending confirmation. Shared product requirements are recorded in `docs/PRD_careKind_AI.md`; recording context, device, intended purpose, integrations and claims remain subject to validation and applicable review.

## Decision rules

1. A credible safety or privacy concern can pause a feature, release or pilot.
2. Wrong-resident attribution, serious factual invention/omission, or cross-customer exposure is treated as a critical incident until triaged.
3. Changes to intended purpose, model/vendor, data use/location, integrations or output format require documented impact review before release.
4. Customer agreements define responsibilities, permitted purposes, subprocessors, locations, retention, security, incident notice, audit evidence and exit/deletion.
5. Claims must match verified evidence and actual regulatory status.
