# Pilot and Release Gate

Do not process identifiable resident data until every applicable required item is evidenced and signed. For a synthetic-data-only pilot, mark resident-data items not applicable and keep all data synthetic.

## Product and regulatory

- [ ] Intended purpose, users, workflow and prohibited uses approved (Product).
- [ ] Written TGA assessment covers device status and ARTG/exemption/notification/adverse-event duties (Product + counsel).
- [ ] Product claims match intended purpose and evidence (CEO/Product).
- [ ] Customer confirms provider/site workflow and clinical governance owner (Customer).

## Privacy and contracts

- [ ] Data-flow map and privacy impact assessment approved (Privacy).
- [ ] Notice, authority/consent and recording workflow approved for context (Privacy + customer).
- [ ] Signed customer and data-processing terms cover purpose, roles, suppliers, locations, AI use/no training, retention, breach notice, support, audit and exit/deletion (Legal).
- [ ] State/territory and offshore access requirements assessed (Privacy).
- [ ] Customer confirms lawful authority/local policy for capture and resident/staff information (Customer).

## Clinical safety and operations

- [ ] Hazard analysis, controls, escalation contacts and stop thresholds approved (Clinical safety).
- [ ] Draft label, human approval, correction history and no silent overwrite verified (Product).
- [ ] Representative workflow validation completed; critical-error results reviewed (Clinical safety).
- [ ] Wrong resident, unclear audio, outage, duplicate, correction and wrong-chart paths validated (Product).
- [ ] Staff training, manual fallback and incident reporting path ready (Customer).

## Security

- [ ] AI/cloud supplier and subprocessor review complete (Security).
- [ ] Access controls, MFA, tenant isolation, encryption, logs, backup, deletion and vulnerability practices verified (Security).
- [ ] Incident response and customer/supplier contacts exercised (Security + Privacy).
- [ ] Support access is least-privilege, time-limited, approved and logged (Security).
- [ ] Insurance and service support reviewed for pilot scope (CEO).

## Pilot plan and approval

- [ ] Scope, sites, users, dates, exclusions, sample size and success/stop criteria agreed (Customer + Clinical safety).
- [ ] Retention and deletion dates cover audio, outputs, evaluation extracts and backups (Privacy + customer).
- [ ] Measures cover efficiency, correction burden, omissions/inventions, subgroup performance and incidents (Product + Clinical safety).
- [ ] Written go decision, conditions and rollback owner recorded (CEO).
