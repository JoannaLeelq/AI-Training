# Australian compliance and paperwork research

Input: relevant features, care setting, provider category and state/territory. Missing context may be documented as assumptions; do not declare legal applicability settled without it.

1. Check existing compliance register and research, then verify current official legislation/regulator guidance. Separate Commonwealth provider duties, software-vendor duties, state/territory rules and recommended controls.
2. Cover only relevant areas: privacy/health records, recording/authority, aged-care records, clinical governance, TGA intended purpose and contracts. Cite effective dates and exact provisions where verified.
3. When comparing jurisdictions, use current state/territory legislation and regulator sources, not only a national summary. Mark uncertain or unverified applicability explicitly.
4. Download requested public official templates/guides to `Marketing & Operations/Research/References/`. Preserve originals; record source URL, retrieval date, version and intended use in the reference index. Check actual download success; never report linked-only material as downloaded.
5. Save findings in `Marketing & Operations/Research/`; update the Company OS register only when the user requested a register update or it is needed to implement the task. Keep legal-review decisions pending without qualified evidence.

Completion: sourced findings and requested references are saved, downloads are accounted for, and gaps/required legal determinations are stated. This workflow generates research, not a compliance attestation.
