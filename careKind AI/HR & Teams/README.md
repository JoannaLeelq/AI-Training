## Suggested structure

- `people/` — role descriptions, hiring plans and workforce directory
- `onboarding/` — induction, access requests, confidentiality and training records
- `policies/` — conduct, privacy, acceptable technology use, leave, WHS and complaints
- `meetings/` — leadership/team agendas, decisions and actions
- `templates/` — reusable interview, onboarding and performance templates

Keep personal and employment information access-controlled. Before hiring, confirm Australian employment, privacy, WHS, superannuation and workers-compensation requirements for the relevant state/territory with an adviser.
