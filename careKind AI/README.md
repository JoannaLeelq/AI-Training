# careKind AI

GitHub repository: [JoannaLeelq/AI-Training](https://github.com/JoannaLeelq/AI-Training). Company files are maintained under `careKind AI/`.

Australia-based aged care technology startup. Shared product requirements and strategy are maintained in [docs / PRD](docs/PRD_careKind_AI.md) for operations, product and development.

- **docs** — shared PRD, product strategy, logic and workflows.
- **APP Development** — architecture, engineering, validation and release work.
- **Marketing & Operations** — company operating system, business operations, customer work, marketing and compliance register.
- **HR & Teams** — people operations, roles, hiring, onboarding and team practices.
- **Feedback** — original user feedback, preserved as raw source material without AI rewriting. See [feedback recording rules](Feedback/README.md).

Shared automation: [workflows](workflows/README.md) defines task execution; [skills](skills/README.md) provides reusable instructions for Claude Code and Codex. The project AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md route requested work into these procedures.

Company standards: [rules](rules/README.md) defines company/operations practices, product and local legal-reference requirements, and SOLID/DRY/KISS coding standards. Product designs and PRDs must record the relevant local law/reference review and requirement mapping.

AI modification history: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) records actual change batches by Codex/Claude, affected files, reasons and validation outcomes under [changelog rules](rules/changelog.md).

Directory context: repository/project roots, departments and substantial existing work areas have AGENTS.md execution guidance, a CLAUDE.md import, and README.md directory context. Actual feature modules receive the same files when created or refactored under [documentation rules](rules/documentation.md); complex functions are documented in their owning module and code comments.

The project is at an early stage. Documents in this workspace are starter materials, not legal advice or proof of compliance. Confirm product scope, applicable laws, contracts and controls with qualified Australian advisers before handling identifiable resident information or launching to customers.
